# Tariq Motors — Multi-page E-bike Website Design Prototype

This is a **multi-page website design prototype**, not just a one-page landing page.

## Pages included
- `index.html` — Home page with 3-slide carousel
- `brands.html` — Brand directory
- `brand-roadking.html` — RoadKing brand page
- `brand-volt.html` — Volt by Road Prince brand page
- `brand-superstar.html` — Superstar brand page
- `bikes.html` — All bikes with brand filter
- `bike-details.html` — Product detail page template
- `about.html` — About Tariq Motors
- `offers.html` — Offers and new arrivals
- `faq.html` — Frequently asked questions
- `contact.html` — Contact and WhatsApp enquiry form

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a browser. Use the header links to navigate the pages.

## Contact details
WhatsApp numbers (+92 300 800 5053 and +92 300 454 2833) and the office coordinates (31.031391, 73.854309) are set at the top of `script.js`. Every WhatsApp button opens a small picker so the customer can choose which number to message. The same details appear in the footer of every page and on the Contact page, which also has an embedded map.

WhatsApp links open a prefilled message. The customer must press Send; the website does not send messages without their action.

## Important product-data note
The bike models and specifications are clearly generic placeholders. Replace them with your actual models and verified manufacturer specifications before publishing. There are **no displayed prices**. Bike-specific WhatsApp messages include the selected brand/model and ask for price, availability, specs, and warranty details.

## Images
The prototype uses CSS-based bike illustrations and gradient hero visuals to establish the design direction. Replace these with official product photography/logos you are permitted to use before launch.
