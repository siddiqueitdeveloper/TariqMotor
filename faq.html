HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions, or contact our team for information about a specific model.

How do I ask for the price of a bike?

Open the bike listing and click WhatsApp Enquiry. A message will be prepared with the brand and model so you can ask us for the latest price.

Why are prices not shown on the website?

We handle price enquiries directly. Send us the bike you are interested in and our team will share current pricing and availability.

Which brands can I browse?

The website currently has sections for RoadKing, Volt by Road Prince, and Superstar. Additional brands can be added as the dealership expands.

Are the specifications on the website final?

The prototype uses placeholder product entries. Before the site is published, the product listings should be updated with verified model names and specifications.

Can I ask about colours and stock?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp enquiry button on a specific bike and ask about available colours, current stock, and collection or delivery options.

What warranty is included?

Warranty coverage depends on the specific model and manufacturer's terms. Contact our team for confirmed details.

Can you add another brand?

Yes. The design is built to accommodate additional brands and models without changing the overall website layout.

Found a bike you like?

Send your selection to our team and ask about price and availability.
